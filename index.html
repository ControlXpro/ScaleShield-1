Most replacements land in 5–10 minutes through the CRM's one-click share. Enterprise and Onyx tiers run a priority queue for near-instant turnaround — our fastest logged replacement was 47 seconds.

Yes — every package lets you attach your own payment methods for ad spend. You keep full control of your billing.
