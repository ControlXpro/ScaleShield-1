Onboarding

From sign-up to scaling — the exact flow.

Here's how a new ScaleShield account moves from a Telegram message all the way to live, scaling campaigns. No surprises, no jargon — just the steps in order.

Step 1 · You

Pick the plan that fits your scale

Tell us your vertical, your daily spend target, and how many accounts you actually need. We'll point you at the right tier — Profile FB ($100), BM3 Verified ($200), BM10 ($550 one-time), Single ($450/mo), Dual ($850/mo), Top Agency ($1,950/mo), Onyx BM2500 ($15,000 one-time), or Agency Unlimited ($35,000/mo). No upselling — just the right fit.

Step 2 · Us

We share the ad accounts to your BM

Once payment is in and we've confirmed your target Business Manager, our team shares the ad accounts directly into your BM with the permissions you requested (Manage campaigns, View performance, Manage Creative Hub, or full Manage ad accounts). No back-and-forth — just a clean handoff.

Step 3 · You

Receive access in your Business Manager

You'll see the new accounts appear inside your BM under the permissions you picked. You also get login credentials to your ScaleShield Client Panel CRM — that's where the day-to-day work happens.

Step 4 · System

First sync into the CRM — ~1–2 hours

The first time we pull your accounts into the CRM dashboard, expect 1–2 hours for everything to populate. We're talking to Meta in batches and writing the live state into the dashboard. Once the first sync is done, every subsequent refresh is fast (every 5 minutes).

Heads up: if the CRM looks half-empty for the first hour, that's expected — it's syncing. Don't trigger anything urgent, just give it a beat.

Step 6 · You

Warm up each account — $2–5 on 5 white-hat ads

Before you push real volume, every ad account needs a warm-up. The recipe is simple:

The warm-up recipe: launch 5 simple, white-hat / neutral ads per account with a tiny budget — $2–5 total spend across them. Let them deliver. This builds trust signals on the asset so Meta doesn't flag your real campaigns later.

Step 7 · You

Slowly start scaling your real offers (BH / GH)

Once warm-up has run and the account has a few dollars of clean spend on it, start launching your real campaigns — BH, GH, nutra, iGaming, finance, peptides, casinos, whatever you actually run. Push the budget up gradually over a few days, not in one jump. Meta is still watching for sudden behavior changes during the first week.

Step 8 · ScaleShield

If something dies with little spend — we replace it free

Sometimes Meta still kills an account early, even with proper warm-up. If an account goes down with $0 or minimal spend, you get a free replacement — just message support. (Subscriptions also include unlimited replacements throughout the billing period regardless of spend.)

Step 9 · Important

About the low spend limits — don't freak out

When you first see your accounts, the daily spend limit will look tiny — like $50, $100, sometimes lower. That's normal. It's how Meta protects new assets.

Day 0 · before warm-up
$50–$100
Low daily spend cap on a fresh account. Meta is watching everything you do for trust signals.
After $2–5 warm-up
$1,000s/day
After a clean warm-up and a few days of normal delivery, the cap typically jumps to thousands per day. Don't push the limit on day 1 — let it grow.
TL;DR: the low limit you see at first is not the limit you'll actually run at. Warm up properly, and the ceiling lifts itself.
