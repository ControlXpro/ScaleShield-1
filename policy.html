Structure

How the structure works.

We hand you a dedicated profile — think of it as an ad-account distributor, not your operating workspace.

⚠️

The one rule inside the profile we give you

We create a profile for you on AdsPower and hand you access. Inside that profile, do not perform any actions except sharing assets out to your own profile. Don't run campaigns, edit settings, or operate inside it. Treat it purely as a distributor. This keeps the source clean and avoids bans and red flags.

🔑

1 · Get the distributor

We share a ready AdsPower profile to you, loaded with your BM and ad accounts.

📤

2 · Share assets out

From that profile, share the ad accounts / BM into your own profile. The only action you take inside it.

🚀

3 · Operate from yours

Run everything from your own profile. The distributor stays untouched, so the assets stay clean.

🛡️ Critical · Do this on day one

Keep your assets safe — always hold a backup profile.

A profile can occasionally go down — a browser sync issue, a lockout, or a problem on your main. If every BM lives in one profile and it goes down, you lose your door in. The fix: keep a second profile with the same BMs shared to it, so access always lives in two places and you can restore everything.

1

Create a second AdsPower profile

After we give you access, create another profile of your own — your dedicated backup. Keep it separate from your main working profile.

2

Share all your BMs to the backup

From your main profile, share every BM (and its ad accounts) to the backup with full access. It should hold a complete copy of access to all your assets.

3

Verify the backup sees everything

Open the backup profile and confirm every BM and ad account is visible and accessible. If anything's missing, share it again.

4

Keep the backup idle

Don't run campaigns from it — it's a safety net, not a second workspace. Leave it untouched so it stays clean and ready.

5

If your main ever goes down

Log into the backup — you still have full access to every BM and ad account. Re-share your assets to a fresh profile and keep operating. Nothing lost.

✅

The result: no single point of failure

With a backup holding the same BMs, even if one profile dies your assets survive — because access lives in two places. Set it up on day one and keep it current whenever you receive new BMs.

FAQ

Policy questions.

What does a BM10 actually include?+
A Business Manager with 10 ad accounts inside. The number in the package name is the number of ad accounts — BM3 = 3, BM10 = 10, and so on.
When do you replace an account?+
Any account that dies on $0 / minimal spend or runs into issues is replaced free. On monthly subscriptions, replacements are unlimited and unconditional — whatever happens, no questions asked.
How do I protect myself if a profile goes down?+
Keep a backup profile. Create a second AdsPower profile and share all your BMs to it, so if your main goes down you still have full access from the backup and can restore your assets — zero downtime.
Why can't I rename the BM or accounts at first?+
Renaming in the first 24–48 hours is a classic suspicion trigger. Keep names exactly as delivered during warm-up; adjust later once the account has built trust.
My daily limit is tiny — is something wrong?+
No. Limits start low by design. Our system raises them quickly as you spend — warm up clean and the ceiling climbs on its own.
