How it works

Four steps. Zero friction.

The whole thing runs over Telegram. No dashboards to set up, no tracking pixels to install — we attribute every referral manually and the receipts are clear.

Step 1 · You apply

Message us on Telegram or WhatsApp

Send us a quick intro — who you are, what audience you have (media buyers, agencies, affiliates, BH/GH community, etc.), and how you plan to refer. We'll approve you and issue a unique referral code tied to your name. Approval usually takes under an hour.

Step 2 · You refer

Send your referral code with every intro

When a prospect contacts us, they include your code (or you intro them directly via Telegram). We log them against your account the moment they reach out. No 30-day cookies, no “last-touch” games — once they're attributed to you, they stay attributed to you.

Pro tip: The simplest move is to make the intro yourself — add us to a Telegram chat with the prospect. That way the attribution is instant and unambiguous.
Step 3 · They buy

Client pays us, we log the sale

The client pays ScaleShield directly (USDT, BTC, or bank wire for big tickets). The moment we log the purchase against your referral, you can see it in your affiliate ledger. We tell you every time a referred client buys — not just at month-end.

Step 4 · You get paid

Commission paid out every month in crypto

We close out commissions on the 1st of every month. Payouts go out in USDT (TRC20 or ERC20) by default — or BTC / LTC / ETH if you prefer. Minimum payout is $50; if your month is smaller, it rolls into the next one. No fees on our side.
