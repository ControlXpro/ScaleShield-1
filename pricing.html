Choose the infrastructure that fits your scale.

From a $100 Facebook profile to a $35,000/mo unlimited enterprise package. Every tier includes 24/7 support — with a clear difference on replacements depending on which model you pick.

↻
Replacement policy — subscription vs one-time

Subscriptions: unlimited replacements throughout the billing period — if any account dies, we swap it. One-time purchases (Profile FB, BM3 Verified, BM10): free replacement only if the account goes down with $0 or minimal spend. Once you’ve actually used the asset, the one-time warranty ends — that’s the trade-off for the lower entry price and no commitment.

⚠
Important — warm-up required

All ad accounts (and Profile FB) must be warmed up by spending a few dollars first to build trust signals on the asset before scaling. This protects the account and ensures stable performance once you push real volume. We’ll guide you through the right warm-up flow during onboarding.
