1The problem

Atlas Media Group is a D2C portfolio holding 4 brands across skincare, supplements, kitchen gadgets, and pet. By Q2 2025, all 4 brands were converting profitably — but Sarah's team was hitting a different ceiling.

Every new account they shared into their hub BM would push them closer to Meta's 5-share-per-account limit. Every BM that went down (and they did, regularly) took 30–80 accounts with it. The whole portfolio was one accidental policy strike away from collapse.

"We weren't losing money to bans. We were losing sleep to concentration risk. Everything funneled through one BM and we knew it."

2The Onyx solution

Sarah upgraded to Top Agency ($1,995/mo) for 5 whitelisted agency accounts running her highest-spend offers, then layered on Onyx BM2500 ($15,000 one-time) as the operational hub.

Onyx changed the math entirely. Instead of one BM owning 2,500 accounts (single point of failure), her hub BM had 2,500 accounts sourced from distributed source BMs she controlled via ScaleShield's CRM. If any source BM went down, the accounts inside it were instantly transferable to a fresh BM — without losing the campaign history.

The CRM's one-click share-to-BM also bypassed Meta's re-share cap entirely. Her team could now move any account between any of their BMs in real-time as risk profiles shifted.

"Onyx is the only reason we could test 12 offers in parallel without our hub BM ever being a single point of failure. Take away that single feature and we'd be capped at maybe $2M/mo on the same infrastructure." — Sarah K., Founder, Atlas Media Group
