Business model overview

ScaleShield offers a retention-driven subscription structure for long-term clients, plus a frictionless wallet-powered one-time purchase system for buyers who want to acquire BM10 access with no commitment. Both models increase conversion by letting clients work inside one CRM and pay for ads with their own cards.

Model 01

Monthly Subscription

Retention Engine
Recurring / monthly

Clients subscribe to public website packages or a custom package. As long as their subscription stays active, they keep CRM access, managed account visibility, and unlimited free replacements.

  • ✓Works with BM3, single account, dual account, top package, or custom offer
  • ✓Unlimited replacement requests through CRM while subscription is active
  • ✓Best for clients scaling continuously and wanting predictable support
  • ✓Clients can use their own cards to fund ad spend
Model 02

BM10 One-Time Purchase

Fast Transaction
$500 / one-time

Client tops up wallet inside CRM and clicks Buy BM10. The system automatically deducts $500 and sends a BM10 containing 10 agency ad accounts. No commitment and no warranty.

  • ✓No subscription lock-in and no long-term commitment
  • ✓CRM wallet flow automates purchase and delivery
  • ✓Designed for clients who want direct access without recurring plan
  • ✓Clients can still use their own cards to pay for ads

Model 2 — BM10 one-time purchase system

This model turns CRM into a lightweight checkout and fulfillment machine. It gives buyers instant access to a BM10 product without subscription commitment. The value proposition is speed, simplicity, and direct purchasing inside the client portal.

STEP 01

Client logs into CRM

User enters the portal and sees wallet balance, products, and the BM10 purchase option.

STEP 02

Client tops up wallet

Funds are added to the wallet to prepare for product purchase without manual back-and-forth.

STEP 03

Click “Buy BM10”

CRM automatically deducts $500 from wallet balance at the moment of purchase.

STEP 04

BM10 is sent to client

The client receives a BM10 package with 10 agency ad accounts. No warranty, no commitment, one-time transaction.

⚡

Fast conversion

Perfect for buyers who do not want a recurring subscription but want direct access right now.

🪙

Wallet-based automation

Funds, purchase action, and fulfillment all happen inside the CRM, creating a clean automated flow.

📦

Clear product logic

Simple product offer: one payment, one BM10, 10 agency ad accounts, zero commitment.

Important positioning for BM10

Price: $500 one-time. Includes: BM10 with 10 agency ad accounts. Commitment: none. Warranty: none. Payment logic: client can top up wallet inside CRM and buy instantly. Ad spend: client can still use their own cards to pay for ads after receiving access.
